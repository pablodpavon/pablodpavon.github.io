How I'd read a SearchedBy result

Originally posted on LinkedIn on 10 November 2026.

If you run your site through SearchedBy, here's how I'd read the result.

The number on top is how many AI answers named you. That's the one to watch. Zero is common, especially for small businesses and new sites.

Below it is the technical foundation: can AI crawlers reach your pages, read your content, and understand who you are. If something is broken there, fix it. It's the part you fully control, but a good score alone won't get you named.

Then look at who gets named instead of you. Those names tell you who AI thinks your competitors are, and they're often not the ones you'd guess.

One thing I'm careful about: each scan is a sample, not a verdict. AI answers change from day to day, so treat it as a starting point and run it again after you change something.

It's free: searchedby.com

Update, 24 November 2026. Later on the day this post went out, visitors stopped seeing the technical score. The result now opens with what each AI engine said, and the technical side is three plain answers: yes, partly or no.
