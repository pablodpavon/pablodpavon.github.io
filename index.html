FAQ

What people ask before we start.

Something else? Send me a message and I will reply by e-mail.

Do you work with companies in Argentina from Germany?

Yes. I live in Berlin and work remotely with clients in Argentina, Latin America, Europe and the United States. The 4–5 hour difference with Argentina leaves the whole Argentine morning in common; with Europe, the full working day.

Do you work remotely?

Yes, fully remote and in writing: e-mail, shared documents and documented deliveries. Working hours overlap with both Argentina and Europe.

How is work priced?

Per project at a fixed price, or by the hour, always with the scope in writing before starting. If the scope changes, it is quoted separately before any work is done.

Do you use AI?

Yes, it is the core of the work. AI prepares, drafts and flags; I check the result against the source, and people decide. Nothing is sent or deleted without your OK. If your company restricts AI tools, we start with the process review and agree which tools are allowed.

Will AI replace my team?

No. The goal is to take the repetitive part off their desk so they spend their time on judgement, the business and clients. Each change has an owner and one number to watch.

How do you handle confidentiality?

I sign an NDA when needed, work with the minimum data required, anonymised where possible, and never mix data between clients.

Which tools do you use?

Claude and Claude Code, Google Workspace (Gmail, Drive, Sheets), Microsoft 365, WhatsApp exports, and Python where code is needed. I adapt to what your team already uses, so it can maintain it without me.

What do you need to get started?

Two lines on the task that repeats most and who does it. If you use Claude or ChatGPT, I send you a task so your AI writes the inventory.
