Context

More and more people ask an AI assistant instead of searching on Google. A business can rank well on Google and never show up in those answers, without knowing it.

I wanted to test two things: whether I could take a real product to production with AI writing almost all of the code, and whether that visibility could be measured honestly.

How it works

Crawl the site21 automated checks in the free scan: AI bot access, structured data, content in HTML, entity consistency.
Build the questionsThe scan detects the business type and builds the questions a real customer would ask, with the city for a local business.
Ask the AI enginesThe questions go to the engines by API: ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude and Google AI Overviews and AI Mode, depending on the market.
Classify and explainWhether the business is mentioned, cited or missing, and who shows up instead. A single answer is never presented as a score.

What the AI did and what I did

The code (Python, FastAPI, SQLite, a Hetzner server behind Cloudflare) was written by Claude Code. I wrote the method, the scoring and disclosure rules, the wording of every finding and the acceptance criteria.

With AI, the bottleneck moves from writing code to defining what is correct. That is analyst work.
