Who it is for

  • Teams of 3 to 50 people where everything goes through the owner or one person.
  • The same report, statement or summary is built by hand every month.
  • Information from the field or from clients arrives late, on WhatsApp or by email, and someone sorts it out later.
  • Several spreadsheets hold the same data, and none is up to date.
  • You already use ChatGPT or Claude, but everyone in their own way, and nothing tells you when something breaks.

How I work

Your AI does the inventory.If you already use Claude or ChatGPT, I give you a task so your own AI writes down how you work today: processes, spreadsheets, automations, what broke. If you don't, we do it on a one-hour call.
I review it area by area, and every finding is checked.I separate what works from what doesn't, with evidence from your own files. A second, independent review checks the conclusions before they reach you.
You get a few decisions, not an endless report.Seven at most, each with a recommended option. You answer "ok 1, 3" and your AI applies only what you approved.
We set it up and measure it.Everything in one place, sending blocked by default, and a backup every night. Every change has its number: how long it takes today and how long after.

What I don't automate

Your signature, professional judgement, deletions, or anything sent to your clients without your OK. AI prepares and flags; a person decides.

Pilot case

A construction supervision firm in Israel that delivers a monthly technical report per site. Its AI did the inventory in about twelve hours. The review found what worked, the report engine, and what didn't:

  • field material arrived days late;
  • no automation said when it failed;
  • everything depended on one computer.

The next day the owner had seven decisions and a staged plan. Measured results are not in yet; I will publish them when they exist.

FAQ

Do I need Claude?

Not to start: I work with what you use. To put everything in one place I use and recommend Claude Code, in your account and on your computer.

What about my data and my clients' data?

I don't need passwords. The inventory is done with third-party details replaced, and anything legal goes as questions for your lawyer.

How much of my time does it take?

Answering a few questions, pasting one task into your AI, and deciding. During the setup, a few hours spread over two or three weeks.

What if you're not around later?

Everything stays in your account, backed up and documented so someone else can carry on.

What does it cost?

A fixed price per stage, agreed in writing before we start.
